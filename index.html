Bio

I am in the class of 2026 at Colby College studying music-interdisciplinary computation, where I gained experience in music composition, trombone performance, and computer programming.

During my undergraduate studies, I have written pieces for various instruments like percussion quintet and solo trombone with electronics, and I have performed in the Colby orchestra, wind ensemble, and trombone ensemble.

Proficient in FL Studio, Ableton Live, and MuseScore, and have worked with Python, Java, and Arduino.

I am currently pursuing a master's degree program in music composition to deepen my music making skills and to explore more ways to imbed technology into music.
